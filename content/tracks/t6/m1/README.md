# T6.M1 What a Qubit Is Made Of

Every claim later tracks make about connectivity, gate duration and error rates traces back to a physical choice made here. This module walks eight realisations: transmons and their Josephson junctions, dispersive readout through a coupled resonator, trapped ions entangled through shared motional modes, QCCD shuttling between trap zones, photonic dual-rail encodings joined by fusion measurements, neutral atoms held apart by Rydberg blockade, gate-defined spin qubits in silicon and germanium, and the contested case for Majorana-based topological qubits. Each is reduced to the same three questions: what is the two-level system, how are two of them entangled, and which engineering constraint dominates. The checkpoint tests that comparison, not trivia.
