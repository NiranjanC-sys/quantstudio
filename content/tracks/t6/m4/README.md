# T6.M4 Cryogenics and Wiring

Superconducting qubits operate where their own transition energy is large compared with the available thermal energy, which for a 5 GHz qubit means tens of millikelvin. This module follows the stages of a dilution refrigerator from the 50 K plate down to the mixing chamber, then treats the cooling power at the coldest stage as a hard budget that attenuators and coaxial lines must fit inside. The final lesson states the scaling problem plainly: at one or more control lines per qubit, wiring rather than qubit count is the binding constraint, and readout multiplexing and cryogenic control electronics are answers that carry their own costs.
