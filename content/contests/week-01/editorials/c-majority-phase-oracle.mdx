## The intended solution

Three Hadamards, then three CZ gates, one on each pair of wires. Six ops, depth
4, three two-qubit gates, and not a Toffoli in sight.

The identity that makes it work is

$$
\mathrm{MAJ}(x_0, x_1, x_2) = x_0 x_1 \oplus x_1 x_2 \oplus x_0 x_2 .
$$

Check it on the weight of $x$. If no bits are set, no product is set and both
sides are 0. If one bit is set, every product still has a zero factor, so both
sides are 0. If two bits are set, exactly one of the three products is 1, the
XOR is 1, and majority is 1. If all three are set, all three products are 1, the
XOR of three ones is 1, and majority is 1. So the two functions agree on all
eight inputs.

Now push it into the phase. $(-1)^{a \oplus b} = (-1)^a (-1)^b$, so

$$
(-1)^{\mathrm{MAJ}(x)} = (-1)^{x_0 x_1}\,(-1)^{x_1 x_2}\,(-1)^{x_0 x_2},
$$

and each factor is precisely what a CZ on that pair of wires applies. The oracle
factorises into three commuting two-qubit diagonal gates.

The XOR step is the part worth remembering. Over the integers,
$x_0x_1 + x_1x_2 + x_0x_2$ takes the values 0, 0, 1 and 3, so it is **not** equal
to majority as an integer. It is equal modulo 2, and modulo 2 is all a sign
needs.

## Why the naive answer is too deep

The generic phase-oracle recipe is: allocate an ancilla in $\ket{-}$, compute
$f(x)$ into it reversibly, and uncompute the scratch. For majority the standard
reversible circuit is three Toffolis into the ancilla, one per pair, then three
more to uncompute the scratch bits, on a register that is now four or five wires
wide. Every Toffoli is itself seven $T$ gates. The result is a circuit an order
of magnitude larger than three CZs, and it violates all three of this problem's
constraints at once: the ancilla, the three-wire gates, and the two-qubit gate
budget.

The lesson generalises well beyond majority. Any Boolean function whose
algebraic normal form has degree 2 is a product of CZ gates and single-qubit
$Z$ gates, and needs no ancilla at all. Degree 3 is where Toffolis become
unavoidable.

## Common wrong answers

Applying the three CZ gates without the Hadamard layer leaves $\ket{000}$
untouched, since it is a $+1$ eigenstate of every CZ. Applying `ccz` gives
$(-1)^{x_0x_1x_2}$, which is the AND of the three bits, not the majority: it
flips the sign of $\ket{111}$ only, and leaves $\ket{011}$, $\ket{101}$ and
$\ket{110}$ positive. Adding single-qubit `z` gates on top of the three CZs adds
the linear terms $(-1)^{x_0 + x_1 + x_2}$, which flips four signs that should
not move.
