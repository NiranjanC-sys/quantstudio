Write `h` and `cx` in a notebook and something downstream rewrites them, because
a fixed-frequency transmon does not know those words. What it knows is a virtual
$R_z$, which costs nothing because it is a change of reference frame in the
control electronics, a calibrated $\sqrt{X}$ pulse, and one two-qubit
interaction. This problem asks you to do that rewrite by hand, once, on the
smallest circuit that has anything to rewrite.

## Task

Return a two-qubit circuit that starts from $\ket{00}$ and produces the Bell
state

$$
\ket{\Phi^+} = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt 2}\left(\ket{00} + \ket{11}\right)
$$

up to a global phase, using only the gates `rz`, `sx` and `cz`, with at most one
`cz`.

## Input and output contract

Implement one function, taking no arguments:

```python
def solve() -> dict:
    """Return a RedQuant circuit IR, version 1."""
```

Ops are applied in list order. Angles go in `params` in radians:

```python
{"id": "h0a", "gate": "rz", "targets": [0], "params": [1.5707963267948966]}
```

The two identities you need are

$$
H = R_z(\pi/2)\,\sqrt{X}\,R_z(\pi/2) \quad \text{up to a global phase},
$$

reading right to left as matrices, that is $R_z$ applied first in circuit order,
and

$$
\mathrm{CNOT}(0 \to 1) = (I \otimes H)\,\mathrm{CZ}\,(I \otimes H).
$$

Here $\sqrt{X}$ is the `sx` gate, $\tfrac12\begin{pmatrix} 1+i & 1-i \\ 1-i & 1+i \end{pmatrix}$.

Qubit 0 is the most significant bit, so $\ket{00}$ and $\ket{11}$ are basis
indices 0 and 3.

## Worked example

The reference solution compiles three Hadamards, one on qubit 0 to make the
superposition and two around the `cz` to turn it into a CNOT, and leaves the `cz`
alone. That is nine single-qubit gates, one two-qubit gate, 10 ops and depth 7.
Running it gives:

```text
|00> (-0.5-0.5j)
|11> (-0.5-0.5j)
|<Bell|psi>| = 1.0
```

Both amplitudes are $\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}e^{-3i\pi/4}$, so the overlap with the
ideal Bell state has modulus exactly 1 and the state is correct. The phase is the
price of the $H$ identity holding only up to a factor of $e^{-i\pi/4}$, paid
three times over, and it is unobservable.

## Constraints

- `qubits` is exactly 2 and `clbits` is exactly 0.
- Allowed gates: `rz`, `sx`, `cz`. Nothing else. `h`, `x` and `cx` are all rejected.
- At most one `cz`.
- At most twelve single-qubit gates, so a per-gate rewrite that never simplifies still fits, but a brute-force search that emits dozens does not.

## Scoring

one penalty minute per rejected submission.
